# Foundations PL4 1.12.2 0.2a-legacy-preview.2 — legacy fluid transport

**Experimental/alpha legacy subset. Further API testing is still required.**

Adds native sided fluid export/import nodes, a built-in 16,000 mB tank, vanilla water/lava bucket interactions, and sealed fluid recovery cells for fluid left in tanks or exporter buffers when removed. No third-party tank or tooltip mod is required. The existing command recipe browser lists the actual new recipes.

Craft the tank with iron at the four corners and glass at the four side centers. Combine a legacy item exporter and an empty bucket for a fluid exporter; shapelessly convert it to an importer or back. Join exporters/importers with your cables and sneak-click their tank-facing sides. Right-click a tank with a water/lava bucket or empty bucket; fluid variants carrying NBT are not silently converted into plain vanilla buckets. Right-click your tank with a healthy recovery cell to restore it, including partial restores.

Defaults: 250 mB per exporter every 10 ticks, 16,000 mB tank capacity. network.fluidsEnabled and network.fluidMilliBucketsPerOperation control fluid transport; the existing global transport switch and network size cap remain respected. Item and fluid endpoint roles stay separate. Tanks do not bridge data networks. Already-loaded targets only, owner-separated PL4 tanks, redstone pause, physical/provider identity deduplication and a 64-call fluid-operation budget.

Actual drain/fill calls are marked in flight before execution. Invalid or throwing execute calls leave the buffer quarantined rather than automatically replaying an uncertain transfer. Quarantine and opaque NBT for unavailable fluid registrations survive serialization and recovery-cell drops. Quarantined cells cannot be restored automatically: inspect the provider/server state before any administrative recovery. This does not promise crash-atomic commits across independently saved third-party tanks or safety against providers that mutate during simulation.

Legacy save schema 2 reads prior preview item fields; new fluid fields are additive. Back up worlds before upgrading and do not downgrade fluid-containing worlds. Use matching client/server preview versions. New full-block models use vanilla textures; final tank gauges, dedicated visual design and graphical interfaces are still pending.

Validation gates: previous item/server tests plus the added native fluid scenarios, shared fluid conservation/fault tests, native compilation, reobfuscation and runtime packaging checks. Installed third-party mod combinations, real-client visuals, multiplayer sessions and sustained large-world performance acceptance remain pending. Advanced displays, multipart geometry, wireless storage, energy adapters and complete scripting parity remain unfinished. 1.6.4 remains experimental; 1.7.10 is excluded. No universal power conversion or bundled optimization mod is introduced.
